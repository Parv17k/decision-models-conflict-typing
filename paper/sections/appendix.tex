\section{Protocol Record}
\label{app:protocol}

The development/test split, the prompt and question texts and the combination rules were frozen with recorded file
hashes before any test-set call. Changes after that point were limited to the following, each logged with its reason
before results were computed: (1) Gemini~2.5 Flash with thinking added as a sensitivity check; (2) the answer parser
made lenient for category statements outside the requested JSON, applied to stored answers without new calls (no
strictly formatted answer changed); (3) Gemini~3.8 Flash added at two thinking levels; (4) the cascade, its
operating points and its controls; (5) the diagnostics, with one amendment before any planted run: whole-word
matching and the exclusion of yes/no answers, after substring matching was found to alter unrelated numbers;
(6) a second \jev version and four further decision models, run with the frozen questions; (7) the remaining
five decision models listed by the gateway, completing the census, with the diagnostics extended to every decision
model's flat question, and one amendment before any run: Classify-1 receives the same content as a single text
field, which its API requires; (8) the end-to-end test, specified before any generation call: generator, prompts,
judges, the \nvalid-answer validation sample and its threshold, and a second judge (Gemini~3.8 Flash) to be reported
with the first. Prompts were checked on the development split for errors only; none was changed after scoring. All
runs are reported.

\paragraph{Judge validation.} The \nvalid answers were drawn with a fixed seed, 20 per gold type and about half from
Vanilla, and shown to the annotators with the query, the gold type's description and expected behaviour, and the
answer, without the judge's verdict or the condition. Annotator 2's sheet showed another item's answer for one item;
that label is excluded. The primary judge agrees with the annotators' majority (a tie counting as \emph{follows},
as fixed before labelling) on \eevalacc\%, with annotator 1 on \eevalaccone\% and with annotator 2 on
\eevalacctwo\%; the second judge on \fbvalacc\%, \fbvalaccone\% and \fbvalacctwo\%. Code,
splits, prompts, cached responses and per-query outputs are released.
